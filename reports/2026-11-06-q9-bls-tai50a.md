# Q9: breakout local search on tai50a, against the memetic baseline

Sandbox (4 cores), build `beb9bb6` (includes the stop-at-target fix), a
frozen copy of `qap_advanced`, `--runs 1 --jobs 1` (single-threaded),
`--max-seconds 3600`, seeds 1–5 per arm, 4 runs at a time. The run survived
three sandbox restarts by being restartable (a finished run is recorded and
skipped); runs cut by a restart were repeated from scratch.

Decision rule (`PLAN-problems.md` Q9): if BLS does not reach tai50a's best
known 4,938,796 within 1 hour on 5 seeds, retire the direction.

**0 of 5 reached the best known; median gap 0.45%.** By the rule, the
direction is retired. The plan's note applies: a miss on every seed by
0.24–0.47% is far larger than what the two known deviations from the
paper (first-improvement `SwapDescent` instead of a best-improvement
descent; aspiration against the perturbation's best instead of the global
best) or the parameters recalled from memory are likely to explain.
