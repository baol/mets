## Reading, against the decision rules in `PLAN-problems.md` Q6

* **Aspiration.** 20n² reaches 5/5 like 10n², with a median of 35 s
  against 355 s, and three seeds under 40 s; its median best at 5 minutes is
  already the best known. By the stated rule ("if 20n² also reaches 5/5
  with a lower median than 10n², extend once more") the next level is 40n².
  The spread is wide (12 s to 1,027 s), so the median is the signal, not
  the max.
* **A against B at 10n².** The population (A) reached 4/5, not 5/5, so by
  the rule the Q6 hypothesis holds at n = 50: independent trajectories (B)
  and B with perturbation (C) go on to tai60a and tai80a. Among the seeds
  that reached, A's times (290–456 s) are like B's and C's; the difference
  is the one seed that never left 4,941,410.
* **C against B at 10n².** Both 5/5; C's max (438 s) is far below B's
  (1,549 s), with similar medians. Five seeds cannot separate them; both go
  on.
