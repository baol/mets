# P7 steps 1 and 7: CVRPLIB reader, `Routes` model, independent verifier

Build `HEAD` of this commit; sandbox, one core.

* **Step 7, `scripts/verify_vrp.py`**: recomputes a CVRP/VRPTW solution's
  cost and checks it with none of the solver's code: every customer once,
  capacity, route duration (`DISTANCE`, with service times from
  `SERVICE_TIME` or `SERVICE_TIME_SECTION`), time windows (VRPTW), the
  stated `Cost`. Distances: `EUC_2D` rounded to the nearest integer (X, XL,
  XTW) or real (CMT, Golden; by name unless forced), `EXPLICIT` in the TSPLIB
  formats. Written in Python, as `verify_qap.py`, rather than the plan's
  `examples/verify_vrp.rs`: a second language makes it independent of the
  Rust reader by construction.
* **Step 1, `examples/common/vrp.rs`**: the `.vrp` reader (the same
  conventions) and the `Routes` model: one route per vehicle, per-route
  load, length and duration, the position of each customer, and the
  penalties inside the model, so `evaluate()` is distance + α·excess load +
  β·excess duration (`Penalties`). Time windows are read but not yet in the
  model. `examples/cvrp.rs --check VRP_DIR SOL_DIR` builds the routes of
  every `.sol` and compares.

## Check against every solution received

| set | instances | `verify_vrp.py` | `cvrp --check` (Rust) |
|---|---|---|---|
| DIMACS (ORTEC, Loggi; explicit matrices) | 12 | 12/12 | 12/12 |
| XTW (VRPTW, rounded EUC_2D) | 100 | 100/100, time windows included | 100/100 (distance, load, visits) |
| XL (1,048–10,001 nodes, rounded EUC_2D) | 100 | 100/100 | 100/100 |

Every stated cost recomputes exactly, with no violation, in both programs.
This settles the distance conventions for these sets before any solver
number is computed. CMT and Golden (real distances, route length limits,
service times) are not in the sandbox yet; they are the acceptance test of
the port (step 8) and will be checked the same way when they arrive.
