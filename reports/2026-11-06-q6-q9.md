# Q6 and Q9: code, checks, and the Q9 run

## Q6: `qap_gpu` flags for the tai-a arms (build `2720163`, CPU path `1cd68a8`)

* `--independent` (arm B): no crossover; every member is its own robust tabu
  trajectory, each batch continuing where the member stopped. On the GPU the
  kernel keeps each member's current permutation and tabu table across
  launches (`resume`, with an iteration offset so long-term aspiration ages
  stay right) and tracks its all-time best. The CPU path (`--cpu`)
  continues from the current permutation with a fresh tabu memory.
* `--perturb-stalled` (arm C, implies `--independent`): after a batch that
  did not improve a member's best, k random swaps on its current
  permutation, k uniform in [n/10, n/4].
* `--tenure LO,HI` and `--aspiration X`: Taillard's tenure range (as
  fractions of n) and long-term aspiration age (in n²), on the GPU and CPU
  paths. Defaults 0.9,1.1 and 5. The kernel takes them as `double`: in
  `float`, 0.9 × 150 truncates to 134 instead of 135, which would have
  changed the default behaviour.
* The trace's start line gives `mode=`, `tenure=` and `aspiration=`.
* Review item Q6.1 (build `1cd68a8`): the CPU path now keeps one `State`
  and one `RobustTabuSearch` per member and resumes them across batches
  (`Runner::resume`), so `--cpu` arms B and C are the same algorithm as on
  the GPU; a member the host perturbed keeps its tabu memory.
* Review item Q6.2: the independent loop asserts that no member's best gets
  worse across a resumed batch. With `--cpu` on tai30a, `--iters 20
  --run-to-end`, 8 s: 2,546 batches (`--independent`) and 2,520
  (`--perturb-stalled`), all recounts clean, the assertion held, the batch
  minimum never went up.

Checks: with `--cpu` on tai30a (60 s each, iters 50n), memetic, independent,
independent + perturbation, and independent with tenure 0.5,0.7 and
aspiration 2 all reach the optimum 1,818,146 with clean recounts in every
batch (5, 24, 10 and 21 batches). The GPU path compiles; it has not run.

Rebase note: `f0cfd2f` (cohesive crossover on similarity matrices) arrived
during this work; its crossover selection is kept, inside the memetic
branch of the new main loop.

## Q9: `qap_advanced --method bls` (build `839cc59`)

`IteratedSearch(perm::SwapDescent, perm::directed_perturbation, Always)` on
the plain model.

**A bug found and fixed on the way.** The first version (directed
perturbation as built for Q2) stalled: on tai30a, single-threaded, 1,000,
10,000 cycles all ended at 1,826,230 (0.44%). The directed probability and
the strength moved only when the incumbent cost was exactly the previous
one; with `Always` acceptance the search alternated between a few local
optima of different costs, so every perturbation stayed at minimal strength,
fully directed, and the descent undid it. `DirectedPerturbation` now follows
BLS: directed probability max(exp(−ω/T), P₀) with ω the perturbations since
the best last improved; a strong random perturbation of L_max swaps after T
of them; strength from L₀ = 0.15n, +1 per return to the same optimum, up to
n/2; tenure n. T = 2,500 and P₀ = 0.75 are recalled from the paper, not
checked. After the fix, single-threaded on tai30a: 1,836,340 after 1,000
cycles (0.1 s), 1,825,262 after 10,000 (1 s), the optimum after 9.7 s.

This also changes `--perturbation directed` inside `memetic`, so the Q2
measurement in `2026-11-05-records-round.md` describes the old rule.

**Q9.4: cycles.** `--method bls` now prints its cycles (descent +
perturbation) and cycles per second. Single-threaded on tai30a: about
8,700–8,900 cycles/s.

**A second bug: a best reached by a perturbation was not notified (build
`beb9bb6`).** BLS did not stop at the target: without `--run-to-end` it ran
all 100,000 cycles on tai30a (11.3 s), so its "found … in X s" was the
whole run's time, not the time to target. Cause, in the library: a
perturbation changes the solution through `State::modify_current`, which
records nothing and notifies no one; when the perturbation itself reached a
new best (directed perturbation's aspiration allows it), the descent made
no improving move and the best was taken only when the inner scope closed,
without an `Improved`/`GlobalImproved` event, so `on_global_improvement`
(and the stop at the target) never fired. Fix: `State::record_current`,
called by `IteratedSearch` after each perturbation and by `Memetic` after
each starting point; a test with an inner search that never moves fails
without it. After the fix BLS stops on tai30a after 30,756 cycles, 3.5 s.
Earlier times to target of `memetic`, `ma` and `restarts` could have been
late by the same mechanism when a child or a perturbation was itself a new
best; random perturbations make that rare.

**Run.** The first run (build `839cc59`) was lost when the sandbox machine
restarted 40 minutes in, and it would have been invalid anyway (the stop
bug). Restarted on build `beb9bb6` with a frozen copy of the binary,
restartable (a finished run is recorded in `results.txt` and skipped on a
restart): BLS and `memetic`, 5 seeds each (Q9.2: the baseline now has 5),
single-threaded, 1 hour each, 4 at a time.

**Run (first version, superseded).** Decision rule from `PLAN-problems.md` Q9: if BLS
does not reach tai50a's best known 4,938,796 within 1 hour, 5 seeds,
retire the direction. Each seed single-threaded (`--runs 1 --jobs 1`),
`--max-seconds 3600`; seeds 1–4 in parallel, then seed 5 beside the baseline
arm (`memetic`, the current default, seeds 1–3, same budget and threading).
